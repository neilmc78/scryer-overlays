Format logos drawn on poster badges in place of their labels.

Traced to single-colour vector paths from the PNG artwork in the Silo
server's overlay assets (web/src/assets/overlay-logos/formats), which were
in turn cropped from jmxd/Kometa (overlays/images). That repository states
no licence.

TrueHD and Dolby Vision to Dolby Laboratories; DTS-HD to DTS, Inc.; HDR10+
